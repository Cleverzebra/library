Add to your Dock or Home Screen

Mac, in Safari (macOS Sonoma or later)

  1. Open the library in Safari.
  2. Choose File > Add to Dock, or click the Share button and choose Add to Dock.
  3. Keep or change the name, then click Add. The library opens in its own window from the Dock or Spotlight.

iPhone, in Safari

  1. Open the library in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button. On newer iPhones, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if you see it, then tap Add.

If Add to Home Screen isn’t in the list, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list to add it.

The Dock and Home Screen versions keep their own favorites, separate from Safari. Use Export and Import to copy them across.

About this library

The library looks for new sites on the Cleverzebra GitHub account when you open it (at most once an hour) and when you choose Check for new sites. A site is added once its web address answers. “Last checked” is when the library last looked for sites; it is not a review of what the sites say.

After the first visit, the library opens without an internet connection and shows the last saved list. The sites themselves may still need a connection.
